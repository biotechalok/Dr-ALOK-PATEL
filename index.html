01 / About

Biotechnology for a more sustainable world.

I am an Assistant Professor (Universitetslektor) in Biochemical Process Engineering at Luleå University of Technology, Sweden. My research bridges microbiology, biochemical engineering, metabolic engineering and resource recovery.

My work focuses on cultivating microalgae, thraustochytrids and oleaginous yeasts to produce omega-3 fatty acids, squalene, microbial oils and other valuable products from renewable and waste-derived feedstocks.

I earned my PhD from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee in 2017, studying triacylglycerol-accumulating oleaginous yeasts for biodiesel production.

02 / Research themes

From microbial cells to real-world solutions.

Connecting molecular understanding with scalable and sustainable bioprocesses.

RESEARCH / 01

Omega-3 fatty acids & nutraceuticals

Microbial DHA, tailored lipids, squalene and other high-value compounds from marine microorganisms.

RESEARCH / 02

Microalgae & precision fermentation

Microalgal cultivation, microbial cell factories, process optimization and advanced bioreactor systems.

RESEARCH / 03

Omics & metabolic engineering

Transcriptomics, metabolomics and genetic engineering to understand and enhance microbial biosynthesis.

RESEARCH / 04

Waste-to-value biorefineries

Upcycling fish-processing effluents, forest residues, plastics and other challenging waste streams.

RESEARCH / 05

Renewable energy & biomaterials

Microbial oils, biofuels, biohydrogen and sustainable cellulose-based materials.

RESEARCH / 06

Critical mineral recovery

Microalgae-assisted recovery of lithium and rare earth elements from mining and industrial waters.
