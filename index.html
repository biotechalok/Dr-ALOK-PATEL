01 / My story

Why I do
science.

“Can we turn society's toughest waste challenges into the next generation of sustainable food, fuels and materials?”

My research explores how microalgae, thraustochytrids and oleaginous yeasts can be engineered and cultivated to produce nutraceuticals, omega-3 fatty acids, biofuels and other high-value products from renewable and residual feedstocks.

After completing my PhD in Biosciences and Bioengineering at IIT Roorkee in 2017, I joined Luleå University of Technology. My work connects microbial biotechnology, metabolic engineering and scalable bioprocess design with a circular bioeconomy.

02 / Research themes

Questions that
drive my work.

From microbial cell factories to sustainable resource recovery, my research seeks practical pathways to a low-carbon bioeconomy.

01 / BLUE BIOTECHNOLOGY

Microalgae & microbial oils

How can we enhance DHA, omega-3 fatty acids, squalene and other nutraceuticals through strain selection and process optimization?

02 / PRECISION BIOPROCESSES

Metabolic engineering & multi-omics

How can genetic tools and systems-level insights guide microbial carbon flux toward targeted products?

03 / CIRCULAR ECONOMY

Waste-to-value biorefineries

Can food, forest, plastic and industrial residual streams become feedstocks for high-value microbial products?

04 / ENVIRONMENT

Wastewater & metal recovery

How can salt-tolerant microalgae contribute to wastewater treatment and recovery of critical materials?

05 / SUSTAINABLE ENERGY

Biofuels & biohydrogen

What microbial and process strategies can help produce renewable fuels from waste-derived carbon and CO₂?

06 / NEXT-GENERATION MATERIALS

Biopolymers & sustainable packaging

How can microbial cellulose and other biopolymers replace fossil-based materials?
